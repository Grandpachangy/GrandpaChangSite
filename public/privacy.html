Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

This is a personal streaming page. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and nothing to buy, so there is very little to collect — but "very little" is not "nothing", and this page says exactly what there is.

Who is responsible

GrandpaChang operates this site and decides what is described here. For any question about this page or your data, or to make any of the requests below, contact gc@grandpachang.com.

What is collected automatically

  • Anonymous visit statistics. Page views and performance timings, through Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. These set no cookies and do not identify you or follow you to other sites.
  • Server logs. Vercel, which hosts the site, records request details including IP address as part of delivering pages and protecting against abuse.

The lawful basis for both is legitimate interest — knowing whether the site works and keeping it available. Neither is used to build a profile of you, and neither is sold or shared for advertising.

Embedded content

The stream player, the Twitch chat and the side stream are loaded from itzon.tv and Twitch. When one loads, that service receives your IP address and can set its own cookies, under its own privacy policy rather than this one.

Because of that, none of them load until you allow it. You are asked once, and can change the answer at any time from the "Cookie choices" link in the footer. Declining leaves the rest of the site fully working.

What is stored on your device

No tracking cookies are set by this site. A few small values are kept in your browser's local storage, only to make the page behave the way you asked:

  • consent:embeds — whether you allowed embedded content.
  • perf:choice — whether you turned the animated background on.
  • league:lastAccount — which account was last seen in a game, so the status card can finish showing a result.

These never leave your browser. Clearing your site data removes them, and the site will simply ask again.

What is shown, and whose data it is

The stream status, VODs, League of Legends status and "now playing" track are GrandpaChang's own information, pulled from Twitch, Riot Games and Last.fm. It is not about you and is not connected to your visit.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you can ask for access to any personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent for embedded content at any time — the footer link does that in one click.

You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority if you think something here is wrong.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
